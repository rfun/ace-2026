Besan Ladoo

From the kitchen of Jordan Example (sample)

Revenue Cycle, Salt Lake City

Punjab, India · Diwali · Sweets · Makes 16 · 40 min

Sample recipe✓ Vegetarian⊘ Gluten-free

My grandmother made these every Diwali, and the whole house smelled of roasting gram flour for days. She never measured anything; this is my best attempt at writing her hands down.

Ingredients
  • 2 cupsbesan (gram flour)
  • 1/2 cupghee
  • 3/4 cuppowdered sugar
  • 1/2 tspground cardamom
  • 2 tbspchopped pistachios
Steps
  1. Melt the ghee in a heavy pan over low heat.
  2. Add the besan and roast, stirring constantly, 15-20 minutes until nutty and golden.
  3. Take off the heat and let it cool until just warm.
  4. Stir in the sugar and cardamom.
  5. Shape into walnut-sized balls and press a pistachio piece on top.
